# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CBox is a community project. We want participation to be welcoming and
respectful, regardless of experience, identity, background, or preferred
toolchain. Good-faith disagreement is part of engineering; personal attacks
and harassment are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, discussions, and
  other project spaces.
- Critique code and ideas directly, without insulting the people behind them.
- Assume good intent when reasonable, ask clarifying questions, and respond
  constructively to feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience and help keep technical discussions
  useful and on topic.
- Give credit for others' work and respect privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and
publishing private information without permission. Repeatedly disrupting
discussions or using abusive language toward participants is also not
acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repository issues,
pull requests, discussions, and project-related events or communications. It
also applies when someone is representing CBox or its community in another
space.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainers privately through a channel listed on the CBox GitHub repository.
Please include enough detail to help assess what happened; avoid sharing
someone else's private information beyond what is necessary.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as they can, respect
the privacy of everyone involved, and take action appropriate to the
circumstances. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary
restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Retaliation against
someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in CBox project spaces, you agree to follow this Code of
Conduct and to help keep the community respectful and welcoming.
